# Petgiz SEO Notes

## Positioning

Petgiz presents practical pet essentials for walks, carriers, backpacks and day-trip travel. Product pages should describe actual materials, dimensions, fit guidance, care and intended use without unsupported safety, certification or airline-acceptance claims.

## Product themes

- Adjustable pet harnesses and matching leads
- Soft-sided pet carriers and cat travel bags
- Ventilated pet backpacks
- Pet travel accessories and coordinated sets

## Route intent

| Route | Search intent | Main next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | Discover Petgiz | Explore the collection |
| `/products` | Browse pet essentials | Open a product |
| `/products/:slug` | Compare product details | Ask about the product |
| `/about` | Learn about the brand | Explore the collection |
| `/news-blog/` | Read practical pet travel notes | Read a related guide |
| `/faq` | Find sizing, care and travel answers | Contact Petgiz |
| `/contact` | Get help choosing | Submit a message |

Keep account, administration and internal support routes `noindex`.

## Metadata and structured data

- Use concise titles and descriptions that identify the Petgiz product or topic.
- Canonical origin defaults to `https://www.petgiz.com`; set `VITE_SITE_URL` and `SITE_URL` to the verified production domain before launch.
- Each public product and article gets its own canonical URL and relevant image.
- Product structured data describes the item, size/format and colorway. Do not publish prices or availability until those details are verified.
- FAQ structured data uses published entries only.

## Content and launch

- Keep product dimensions and care details aligned with approved product information.
- Confirm the real business name, contact details and production domain before launch.
- Replace or supplement the original vector product illustrations with approved product photography when available.
- Verify canonical tags, Open Graph previews, JSON-LD, forms, `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` on the production domain.
